    The three versions

      Only the lettering differs between them. The shells and everything inside are the same.

      Take part

      Three ways, each fine by itself.

      1. Enjoy it and share it. It is free to use and share with credit (MIT licence).
      2. Send a measurement. Pick the part in Parts and choose “Confirm a feature”. Describe the measured feature and how you measured it; the maintainer reviews the evidence.
      3. Adjust a part yourself. Edit your own copy in Blender and send a link to it with the measurement it rests on.

      Photos we need

      Body width and depth — square-on front and side, ruler in plane

      Show the body (EXT-010 rear cabinet, EXT-020 upper panel and EXT-030 cassette lid) square-on from the front and side. Put a ruler in the plane of each measured face and include its full edges. These views could resolve conflicting front-width evidence and uncertain body depth. State the exterior version using the lettering.

      Flywheel MEC-011 — face and edge, ruler at rim

      Show MEC-011 square-on to its face and in an edge profile, with a scale beside the rim in the relevant face plane. Keep the rim, thickness and belt grooves visible. These views could resolve the wheel’s rim size, thickness and groove spacing. Identify the player model and exterior version.

      Cassette seating and bay — front and side with cassette position visible

      Show the seated cassette (MEDIA-001) and bay liner/supports (BAY-010, BAY-070, BAY-071 and BAY-072) square-on and from the side. Place a scale beside the relevant seating surface in the same plane. Keep the cassette edges and surrounding bay visible to resolve seating position and clearance. State the exterior version.

      Rail BAY-073, lid EXT-030 and EJECT lever STP-003 — interface close-ups

      Photograph BAY-073 against the rim of EXT-030 with the lid closed and partly open, then the interface with STP-003 at relevant eject positions. Keep a scale in the local interface plane and identify each lid/eject position and exterior version. These views could resolve the real shapes and clearances behind the known model intersections.

      Use “Report a correction” for photo-only evidence, or “Confirm a feature” for a measured feature. GitHub submissions are public: hide serial numbers and personal details, and remove photograph location data. Nothing changes the model or a confirmation level automatically; every submission is reviewed.

      Mouse and touch

      Drag to turn the model. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Right-drag or drag with two fingers to move it.

      The player answers the pointer: click its buttons, its door, its cassette and its battery cover; drag the volume grips; click the tone switch; hold the orange Hot Line button. With the cover open, click a battery to take the pair out and the empty case to put them back. Click a headphone socket or DC IN to plug in, and the plug to pull it out. Everything here is in the Actions menu as well.

      Open Parts and click any part to read about it.

      Keys: Space pauses the motion, 0 resets, Escape closes what is open. With the model in focus, the arrow keys turn it and + and − zoom.

      Sound

      The tape holds the music from the start of a 19-minute side and runs on silently after it; the test tone fills the side. Both play at the motion speed, so slower motion sounds slow and low. Tone and Hot Line are simulations: Hot Line mixes in a generated room signal. No microphone is used.
