What the levels mean
    Take part

    Three ways, each fine by itself.

    1. Enjoy it and share it. It is free to use and share with credit (MIT licence).
    2. Send a measurement. Pick the part in Inspect and choose “Confirm a feature”. Photos or links to photos are enough; the maintainer adjusts the model.
    3. Adjust a part yourself. Edit your own copy in Blender and send a link to it with the measurement it rests on.

    Nothing changes the model by itself: every submission is reviewed.

    About this model

    Explore the Sony TPS-L2 through its component IDs, model dimensions and operating controls. Belts use fixed paths with traveling motion cues. Audio consists of locally generated test signals.

    How to present

    Start the guided tour for a short walkthrough, or use the transport and view controls. Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, and right-drag or two-finger drag to pan. In Operate, click the modeled buttons, drag volume grips or the Tone switch, and hold Hot Line. In Inspect, click a component, then Focus or Isolate.

    Space pauses motion. 0 resets. Escape ends the tour and clears isolation. This page works the same opened from disk or from a web host. Arrow keys rotate the focused model; + and − zoom.

    Release & performance

    Portable Tape Player v__RELEASE_VERSION__. Physical accuracy is limited: no part has been checked against a real unit yet. The README says how to help confirm one.
